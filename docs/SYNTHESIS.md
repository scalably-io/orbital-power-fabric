# Synthesis — towards a testable breakthrough (gates fixed 2026-09-27, before any candidate was scored)

**Goal (Pavle, 2026-09-27):** combine existing, measured knowledge into one architecture that, in theory — with first-principles math and only parts that exist today — shows it could work, and that a concrete experiment can confirm or kill.

## Gates (all five must pass; frozen once committed — amendments below with reasons)
1. **Physics.** A first-principles energy model (docs/DERIVATIONS.md) shows ≥ 10× lower full-system energy per useful operation than NVIDIA B300 at matched precision (FP4 187 / FP8 560 fJ/MAC chip-level, R3-070) for the candidate's target workload, AND states the largest support-overhead multiple (control, calibration, clocking, I/O) it can absorb and still clear 10×. No bound violated: shot noise (D11), kT/C (D12), étendue/brightness (D18), thermal.
2. **Evidence.** Every component has at least one MEASURED datum in `evidence/` (tier current or next). No speculative component on the critical path.
3. **Novelty.** A logged prior-art search finds no published demonstration of the full combination. "Novel" is claimed only for the combination and its analysis, never for parts.
4. **Testability.** A falsification experiment ≤ €20k and ≤ 6 months on an available foundry run or bench, with a numeric pass/fail threshold written down before the experiment.
5. **Relevance.** Connects to the program: AI energy, orbital energy, or light-native data.
Ranking among candidates that pass all gates: predicted gain ÷ test cost.

## Candidates (first screen — E018; more arrive from R7–R10)
- **H1 — Resident-weight analog photonic pipeline.** Non-volatile photonic weights (no hold power, no weight fetch), time-integrating photon→charge read-out (no TIA; laser and read-out shared over N·T MACs), analog detector→modulator links between layers (no DAC, no activation SRAM). Target: workloads whose weights stay resident — fixed models, always-on inference, prefill-scale reuse. Known risks to clear: weight density per mm², re-write energy of non-volatile weights, analog error growth with depth, support overhead (318× in the only measured full system, R3-045).
- **H2 — Light-native processing before detection** (data already arriving as light, e.g. Earth-observation imagery in orbit): compute on the light, read out fewer values, downlink less. Known risk: scene light is spatially incoherent — interferometric meshes do not apply; needs intensity-domain (non-negative) optics and étendue-aware coupling.
- **H3 — Sunlight as the compute light source** (no laser, in orbit). Known risk: brightness theorem caps the power one spatial mode can collect from the Sun (D18).
- **H4 — DC-native solar-to-rack power** (R6): +5–12% end-to-end, current/next tier. Practical, not a breakthrough; kept as the paper's deployable win.
- **H5 — Optics for moving weights, not multiplying them** (awaits R9).

## Evidence fold-in
### R7 — conversion-as-compute (E022)
- H1 element (b) time-integrating read-out: **exists, optical side measured, but only with STREAMED weights** (R11-053…056; with resident weights read-out amortises over the fan-in only, A1) — Netcast 40 aJ/MAC optical at 98.8% MNIST accuracy, one read per 100 MACs, σ_rms 0.005 ≈ 8-bit (R7-001…003); client electrical ≈10 fJ/MAC only as a component estimate (R7-004, STUDY-ASSUMED).
- H1 element (c) receiverless detector→modulator activation: **exists** — NTT femtofarad O-E-O 21.5 ± 3.8 fJ/bit measured in its no-gain digital mode (R7-045); the cascadable gain mode (optical gain 1.8–2.4) costs 60.1 ± 5.7 fJ/bit at 2.5 Gbit/s (R7-046/047) — both binary, per bit; FICONN photodiode→microring 30 fJ per activation (author estimate) in a commercial foundry, 3 matrix + 2 nonlinear layers, >92% test accuracy (R7-037…042). With a TIA the same activation costs ~3 pJ (R7-038) — the 100× the gate removes.
- **No system combining these has a measured wall-plug energy per MAC** — H1's crux (support overhead) is confirmed as the open quantity.
- Chip implication: CORNERSTONE passive + heater run has no photodetector and cspdk has no PD cell → H1's read-out must be tested with off-chip/bonded photodiodes + an integrator on the bench; the MPW #50 graphene beta (£7,000; academic free; device types only in the MPW #50 design guidelines) might add on-chip detection — unverified.

### R9 — data movement (E024)
- **H5 killed for on-package memory:** HBM fetch energy is 74% control logic; optics can only replace the 2–7% interposer I/O and costs more per bit than it saves. Optics' data-movement value is at rack-to-cluster reach (scale-out), not in the memory wall.
- Consequence for H1: resident weights (element a) are not an optimisation — they are the only route around the memory wall for photonic compute; H1's scope is therefore workloads whose weights fit on chip.

## What the evidence says is optimal with TODAY's technology (2026-09-27, from merged claims)
1. **Energy for AI: on the ground, solar + batteries built on site, DC-coupled.** Near-firm solar + storage $104–167/MWh for 97–99.4% of hours (R5-027/028, modelled, Las Vegas); a 90%-solar off-grid AI microgrid $109/MWh (R5-029); PV + battery for 24/7 ≈ $11.8k per continuous kW vs GB300 rack $27–48k per kW of IT (R5-086/085, press estimates). The binding constraint is time, not cost: median 61 months to connect to the grid (R5-042) → on-site generation skips the queue. DC-coupling PV onto an 800 VDC rack bus adds +6.8% vs AC-coupled on-site PV, +12.4% vs grid-delivered PV (R6-122, next-tier hardware); grid-fed 800 VDC +5.2% (R6-121).
2. **Space: not yet the energy layer (verdict v1.2, E028).** Microwave beaming is blocked by transmitter mass in both orbits: LEO needs a transmitter ≥ 147–194 W/kg RF-out, GEO (10 GW, 15-y design life) ≥ 130 W/kg, vs 28 W/kg measured (4.7–6.9×); at today's arrays no transmitter value rescues current-tier LEO. Mirrors: Reflect's filed Earendil-1 system at its filed ~1-y life is not lighter than orbital GPUs even in clear sky; sail-class areal density (flown ACS3, 200 g/m²) is lighter only with ideal coverage, ≥ 52% clear sky AND a purpose-built receiver fully covered with 30%-efficient PV; onto a real tracking farm (5.9% of site light, R5-077) only a bare membrane is lighter (E034), and needs ≥ 0.12–0.24 km² of mirror over each site at once. On cost (V3, launch only, $3,600/kg): orbital GPUs, microwave beaming and sail-class mirrors all exceed the full cost of near-firm ground solar; only component-level membranes (no deployer, no bus) pass. Orbital GPUs reach ~$61 per IT-MWh launch-only at $200/kg (projected ~2035, R1-053).
3. **Photonics today: interconnect, not arithmetic.** Co-packaged optics ~5 pJ/bit vs 15–20 pJ/bit pluggables (R9-030) at rack-to-cluster reach. For LLM decode any engine that streams weights from HBM is capped at 1.9–6.4× a B300 even with a free compute core, depending on the memory access energy per bit (GH200 measured 11.68 pJ/bit incl. control, R3-078, to HBM3E 3.44–5.74, R3-081/082; E033, E034) — below the 10× gate in every case (E028, TGP roofline) — the memory wall, not the arithmetic, sets the cost (E024).
4. **Photonic compute for inference: a negative result (A7).** A resident-weight engine with direct photon→charge read-out beats a B300 by 10× only under idealised assumptions (full-scale precision, an unsourced 1 fF node, 20 dB loss): ≤ 10–21× support overhead allowed. Built from measured parts and asked for precision over real outputs, it leaves ≤ 2.65× at NVFP4 pairing and fails at FP8 with zero overhead; +6 dB of loss removes the rest. The architecture is not new (A5). What would change it: P7, P10, P12.
Inferred parts: the ranking itself is our synthesis; every number above is a sourced claim or a logged experiment.

## Amendments
- **[SUPERSEDED by A4 (geometry breaks power conservation) and under review E031]** **A1 (2026-09-27, E025) — H1 corrected to H1'.** R8-129: time integration requires per-cycle weights, so it cannot pair with resident non-volatile weights. H1' = resident non-volatile weights in a WIDE crossbar (spatial fan-in k, T = 1), direct photon→charge read-out, receiverless detector→modulator links between layers, input DAC only at layer 1. Core 1.86–2.35 fJ/MAC (80–302× below B300); passes the 10× gate only if support overhead ≤ 8–30× core (measured system 318×). Scope: small fixed models (≤ ~1–4 M weights on 3–13 cm² at the density upper bound). Dominant remaining core term: the first-layer DAC — removable only if inputs already arrive as single-mode light (e.g. fibre-borne signals), not scene light (R10).
- **A2 — H2 narrowed (R10):** free-space multimode optical triage before detection for Earth observation; value = on-board energy/deadline, not downlink.
- **A3 — H5 killed (R9):** see Evidence fold-in.
- **A4 (2026-09-27, E028, Codex + Opus reviews) — H1' restated with square layers and matched precision.** (i) Power conservation: a passive single-wavelength k→n network with n < k delivers ≤ n/k of its light to the outputs, so the laser amortises over min(n, k) (D13 correction 3) — the n = 128, k = 4096 geometry of A1 is replaced by square, chainable N × N layers. (ii) Precision pairs: 5-bit read-out is compared with NVFP4 only and 8-bit read-out with FP8 (photons ×64); equal model accuracy is not shown (P10). (iii) Photonic weight fetch = HBM + own SRAM, and the GPU decode reference is B300 board power at max TGP on the roofline (no double-counted HBM). Result (`scripts/efficiency_v2.py`, pinned by `tests/test_efficiency_v2.py`): with photon→charge read-out, core 0.91 fJ/MAC (1024², FP4) / 5.42 fJ/MAC (1024², FP8) / 3.63 (256², FP4) / 21.7 (256², FP8); support overhead allowed for 10× = 20.6 / 10.3 / 5.2 / 2.6×. With a conventional (NEP-limited) receiver H1' fails gate 1 at every size (≤ 1.8×) — the direct photon→charge read-out is necessary, not optional. At 30 dB optical loss instead of 20 dB the best case falls to 4.2×. Every figure is an optical-core budget; none is a system gain.
- **A6 (2026-09-27, E031, second fresh-context review of H1' — DO-NOT-SHIP, decision pending with Pavle).** The A4 numbers reproduce exactly, but the physics under them does not hold: (B1) b-bit precision is defined against the largest possible N-term sum, so typical outputs (σ ≈ FS/(3√N)) sit near 0.17 LSB at 5-bit, N = 1024; requiring b bits over the real output distribution removes the 1/N laser amortisation (FP4 support 3.6–6.6×, FP8 core only 1.9–3.8× below B300) — the favourable definition is supported only by network-level simulations (R12-030), so this is a bracket tied to P10; (B2) a square single-wavelength layer reaches the n/k power bound only as an ideal unitary — SVD meshes of measured NV elements lose hundreds of dB (R8-126), incoherent crossbars get no amortisation, WDM needs N channels; (B3) the 1 fF / QE 1 / 1,550 nm node is unsourced — the measured-parts node R8-128 (2 fF, QE 0.91, 1,310 nm) gives FP8 8.4×; (B4) 20 dB loss at 1024² is unsourced (R3-037 PROJECTED) and loss does not grow with N in the model. Majors: O-E-O operating point (R7-046 at 6.4 fJ/bit into its PD), ring weights need resonance locking (R8-103), NEP scaling ×8 not ×64 (R3-107 notes), ADC loading of a 1 fF node, effective bits. R3-045's 318× is a ratio of efficiencies (262 vs 0.81 TOPS/W) and stands, but its core excludes laser and ADCs, so it is an upper bound on like-for-like overhead.
- **A5 (2026-09-27, R11) — gate 3 (novelty): the architecture is NOT novel.** No single document has all four elements in a non-spiking engine with a receiverless modulator activation, but three triples exist (MIT US10268232B2, prio 2016; MIT US11546077B2, prio 2021, static zero-power weights + charge summation + receiverless PD→modulator used for summation; George et al. 2019, simulated), and UC US11144821B2 (2017) has all four in spiking form (R11-001…019). Built hardware stops at pairs/triples (PDONN, FICONN, ACCEL). Measured retention of non-volatile photonic weights is hours (R11-060 and dossier). What we claim: the first-principles energy budget with power conservation and matched precision, and a falsifiable measurement target (support overhead ≤ 2.6–21×, P7/P8) — not the architecture. PDONN's 10.57 W / 121.7 pJ/OP is a calculated tally, not a meter reading (R11-035…037).
- **A7 (2026-09-27, E032, Pavle chose option A: publish H1' as a bracketed negative result).** Correction ladder at 1024², each step one sourced change, cumulative (`scripts/efficiency_v2.py::ladder`, pinned by tests): idealised A4 → + measured-parts detector node (R8-128: 2 fF / 10.5 fF, QE 0.91, 1,310 nm) → + O-E-O at its measured operating point (6.4 fJ into its photodiode, R7-046) → + precision over the output distribution (D21, Monte Carlo-tested) → + buffer before the ADC (R3-062). Support overhead allowed for 10×: NVFP4 20.6 → 12.2 → 5.1 → 2.76 → 2.65×; FP8 10.3 → 8.4 → 8.4 → 0.58 → 0.57×; at 26 dB loss NVFP4 0.73×. Verdict: gate 1 FAILS for H1' with measured parts; it passes only in the idealised bracket whose favourable precision definition is supported by network-level simulations alone (R12-030). The NEP receiver scaling was corrected to ×2 per bit (R3-107 notes).
